# WeHER: Women Healthcare AI Model

*Overview*

WeHER is an AI-powered healthcare assistant designed specifically for women's health. It provides predictive analysis for conditions like PCOS, anemia, obesity, and mental health disorders based on user inputs. The model also offers personalized health recommendations, lifestyle changes, and precautionary measures.

*Features:*
1) Disease Prediction: Identifies risks of PCOS, anemia, obesity, and mental health disorders.
2) Personalized Health Insights: Provides recommendations based on user input.
3) Symptom-based Analysis: Suggests potential health conditions based on symptoms.
4) AI-powered Recommendations: Offers dietary and lifestyle changes based on risk assessment.

*Dataset Components*

1) Demographic Data: Age, weight, BMI, height, and lifestyle habits.
2) Medical History: Previous diagnoses, family history of diseases, menstrual cycle details.
3) Symptoms & Test Results:
  i) PCOS: Irregular periods, acne, hair growth, hormone levels.
  ii) Anemia: Hemoglobin levels, RBC count, iron levels, fatigue symptoms.
  iii) Obesity: BMI, diet patterns, physical activity level.
  iv) Mental Health: Stress levels, anxiety, mood swings.
